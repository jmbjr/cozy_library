# Cozy Library — Prototype Use Cases

## UC-01 Begin an unknown world
The player sees an empty shelf, a hazy world outside, and three titled candidate books.

## UC-02 Choose a book
The player reads the three titles and chooses one that sounds interesting. The chosen book moves to the shelf and its short contents are revealed.

## UC-03 Refresh the offers
Of the two books not selected, one remains. The other returns to circulation and may reappear later. Two new books are drawn into the open positions, restoring three offers.

## UC-04 Reveal the world
Knowledge unlocked by shelved books changes the outside scene. Multiple facts can accumulate and combine.

## UC-05 Complete a world
When every shelf slot is filled, the world is considered documented/completed. Future versions may archive it and travel to another world.
